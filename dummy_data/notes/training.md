## AI Training — Session 1 (Completed)

- Delivered to Golden Eagle sales group.
- Focused on how to use AI beyond just writing emails — prompt engineering, being specific in requests, handling negative customer communication and reframing it positively.
- Feedback from attendees at the end: some felt it was "just ChatGPT training." Tammy's read: this is because most people default to casual chatting with AI and don't appreciate the nuance of structured prompting.
- Tammy's suggested improvements for future sessions:
  - Adjust example pricing in demos to appear more real-world / higher (currently feels low).
  - Open with an explicit framing: "We've met with all of you, this is 101 training, and we are building something similar for the CRM — the AI portion, currently focused on sales but will go across the company."
  - Tell attendees upfront: "You're going to be presenting to us; we're going to be presenting to leadership what we can do for you. That's why we want you to have this basis."
  - Acknowledge that some attendees may find it basic, but encourage them to listen for the takeaways.
- Attendee AI familiarity ranged widely: from "I've hardly ever used it" to "I use it a couple of times a day for emails."
- Daniel was noted as effective at throwing in live examples when the group went silent.
- Time and example quality were both rated positively by Tammy.
