## Social Media Management — Current State

- Social media has historically been managed individually by each person — no collaborative or centralized approach. Goal is to move toward **one consistent message** posted across multiple accounts on a coordinated schedule.
- Chelsea is now taking over social media management for the sales team (5 accounts) in addition to two other employee accounts.

## Social Media Scheduling Tool Evaluation

- **Buffer** (free): currently in use; supports 2 accounts; ~10 posts per account queued at a time. Adequate for LinkedIn (2x/week cadence) but not for Pinterest or multi-account management.
- **Metricool** (paid, ~$20/month): evaluated by Chelsea as the preferred upgrade path:
  - Supports **5 accounts** — enough to cover the entire sales team.
  - **Unlimited scheduling** per platform.
  - **Heat map** feature: shows best time of day per platform for maximum views.
  - **Analytics**: deeper performance tracking to identify what content is working.
