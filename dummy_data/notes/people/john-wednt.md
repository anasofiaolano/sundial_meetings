# john wednt

**Role:** Senior Sales Advisor

## Background
- Has been at Golden Eagle for approximately 10 years (based on context of company evolution discussion).
- Has observed the company's shift from smaller, cabin-type homes primarily in the Midwest toward larger, higher-caliber, more custom-finish homes across a broader geographic footprint.
- Describes the home size as roughly double what it used to be in terms of square footage.

## Day-to-Day Workflow
- Currently working with approximately **10–15 active clients** — either in plan service (design phase) or actively building.
- Maintains a separate pool of approximately **100 clients** in the CRM, tracked via an aging report. These contacts range from 2–5+ years out and are followed up on a rolling basis.
- Daily routine: check emails first, then work through the aging report to stay in contact with longer-horizon leads.
- Estimates approximately **40% of his time** is spent on active plan-service or actively-building clients. The remaining ~60% is split across incoming emails, new phone calls, and aging report follow-ups.
- Believes there is a cap on how many active clients can be managed well — beyond a certain number, client attention suffers and deals may be lost. Has not quantified the exact cap.
- Uses Zoom meetings with clients to review floor plan revisions; calls these sessions "redlines" — sketching out desired changes, then submitting to the design team.

## Lead Handling & Follow-Up
- Inbound calls arrive raw — no pre-submitted information from leads since the CRM's request-for-information web form was disabled by IT (due to spam issues; the spam was not addressed, so the feature was simply turned off).
- Initial calls are qualification-focused: budget, location, time frame, home preferences. Goal: determine if the prospect should enter the CRM at all.
- Tailors follow-up outreach to the client's specific situation — e.g., "checking in on whether you found land" vs. "here's a new YouTube video" depending on where the prospect is in their journey.
- Acknowledges a survival-of-the-fittest dynamic for new inbound calls: reps compete to grab the phone or email as fast as possible because of individual quotas.

## CRM Usage
- Calls are not recorded; John takes notes manually and enters them into the CRM.
- CRM is a home-built system — **no ability to attach files or documents to notes**. If a client sent an attachment, it lives only in email; cross-referencing requires going back to the email inbox to find it. Identified this as a pain point.
- For active clients (those he knows well), relies mostly on memory rather than re-reading CRM notes. For longer-horizon contacts (~100 pool), re-reads notes before reaching out to re-familiarize himself with their story and make outreach feel personal.

## Phone & Communication Systems
- Uses **Elevate** phone system; has the app on his personal cell phone so calls, texts, and SMS come through using the office number (not his personal number).
- Described the Elevate migration as smooth — notably, reps no longer have to be in the office on Saturdays since calls ring directly to their cell phones.
- Communication channels: office phone (via Elevate app), email. Web-based CRM lead form is currently disabled.

## Sales Process
- Standard qualification: time frame ("are you within two years?"), home preferences, location, ballpark budget.
- The **two-year mark** is the explicit threshold: prospects planning to build within two years are engaged actively; those further out are kept in the longer-horizon pool.
- Prospects within the two-year window move to a ballpark conversation.
- If they proceed further, they enter **plan service** — a deposit is paid and the design process begins.
- During plan service, John manages document collection from the client (survey, topo map, soils report, etc.) and reminds them of outstanding items in every communication.
- Describes proactively reminding clients of what's still needed as a key discipline to avoid standstills.

## Quota
- **Senior Sales Advisors are expected to hit $4.7 million** in sold invoices (top-line revenue, not profit).
- Describes the sales culture as hungry but not cutthroat — collaborative internally, competitive on inbound call grabs.

## Team Culture & Collaboration
- Sales reps collaborate informally: bouncing ideas on how to close deals, sharing builder recommendations in specific geographies, helping each other problem-solve.
- No formal weekly pipeline review; sales meetings are approximately **quarterly**.
- John sees value in more frequent structured sales meetings but acknowledges the current cadence is quarterly.

## AI & Technology Familiarity
- Self-describes as "not real super techy."
- Has used **ChatGPT** for: cleaning up email responses to clients, and generating blog content for the Golden Eagle website (elaborating on a topic idea).
- Expressed support for call recording + automatic note entry as a valuable tool — would save re-entry time and improve accuracy compared to recalling a 90-minute conversation from memory.
- No strong current pain points volunteered; open to seeing what improvements are possible once the concept is more concrete.
