## Referral / Builder Network Program
- Golden Eagle maintains a database of builders across the country to provide to clients who need someone to build their home. As more homes are sold, builders get tied up, creating a need for more builders in more geographic areas.
- Golden Eagle has a referral program: if a realtor, builder, or other industry professional refers a qualified prospect who ultimately builds, Golden Eagle pays that referral a reward. The goal is to build a large nationwide "army" of realtors, builders, and other professionals who send qualified leads in exchange for a referral fee.
- Lucas's target: this army could boost sales by approximately **25%**.
- Current outreach approach: primarily manual Google searches and noticing realtor names on Google Maps when looking up a client's property location. Low success rate so far — main barrier is getting through to prospects who screen calls or treat outreach as spam.
- Golden Eagle does **not** do business in California.
- Priority states/regions for this program: the Carolinas (hot for the last several years consistently), Colorado, Wyoming, Montana, and other mountainous areas with great views. State popularity varies year to year with no fixed pattern — could be Vermont one year, Maine or Pennsylvania the next.
